Money is easier to manage when everything has its place.

Soma is a digital money-planning product that brings personal money management and shared-expense tracking together. Organise income, everyday spending, budgets, bills and savings goals, while keeping a clear record of costs you share with a household, partner, flatmates or a group.

Explore Soma

Two sides of everyday money

Personal money planning

Record income and spending, organise expenses into categories, set monthly budgets, track bill due dates and payment status, and monitor savings goals. Review summaries and export reports for your records.

Shared-expense tracking

Create separate spaces for a household, couple, trip or group. Record who paid, split expenses between selected people, review member balances and suggested settlements, and keep a settlement history.

A working early-stage product

Soma is independently developed by its founder. The current version stores personal and shared records in the browser being used; it does not currently provide account-based access, cross-device synchronisation, bank connections or payment processing. Shared spaces help one person organise group expenses; other members do not yet access the same space from their own devices. These are current product boundaries, not claims about future functionality.

Soma is a money-planning tool, not a bank or financial adviser. Figures and balances depend on information entered by the user.
