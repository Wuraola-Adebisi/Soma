# Soma

Soma is a web-based money-planning product that brings personal money management and shared-expense tracking together. It is being independently built by Wuraola Eunice Adebisi.

People can organise personal income, spending, budgets, bills and savings goals, while also keeping a separate record of costs shared with a household, partner, flatmates or another group.

## Personal money

- Record income and everyday spending.
- Build a budget around income and priorities.
- Track bills, due dates and payment status.
- Set savings goals and monitor progress.
- Review summaries and export reports.

## Shared expenses

- Create separate spaces for a household, couple, flatmates or another group.
- Add people by name and email label.
- Record shared expenses, choose who paid and split each expense equally among selected people.
- Review member balances and suggested settlements.
- Record settlements after money has changed hands.
- Export a statement with expense splits, balances and settlement history.

The shared-expense tools currently help one person keep an organised record for a group. Other people are not invited into the space and cannot access it from their own devices. Soma does not initiate payments.

## Current product stage

Soma is a working MVP. Personal and shared records are kept in the browser being used and are not synchronised across devices or connected to a Soma account. Keep separate copies of important records. The product does not connect to bank accounts, import transactions or independently verify financial information.

The roadmap includes individual access to shared spaces and a consistent experience across devices. These are planned improvements, not current features.

## Business and product direction

Soma is being developed as a digital software product for people who want a clearer way to organise personal finances and shared household costs. Its revenue model is still being evaluated; no pricing or paid plan is currently being presented as available.

## Styled reports

Soma generates report layouts for supported personal planner pages and the shared-expense ledger. Reports can be printed or saved as PDF, or downloaded as HTML. They are personal records, not bank-issued statements or proof of payment.

## Run it

```
npm install
npm run dev
npm run build
npm run preview
```

Open `/app` for the planner. `/app#shared` opens Shared money directly.
